THE PHYSGUN

Grab, swing, rotate and freeze anything with a bending gravity beam — grid snapping and angle snapping included. Every building in the world is built from real physical objects.

SURVIVE THE ISLAND

Hunger, thirst, stamina, fall damage. Chop trees that really fall, mine, forage berries, farm planters, cook stew over a campfire — or starve like a true hobo.

🧱

BUILD FREELY

Craft walls, doors that swing, storage boxes with real inventories — drop them into the world and freeze them in place. Trust your friends; everyone else keeps their hands off.

📦

SUPPLY DROPS

Every few minutes a cache of salvage cores and bottle caps lands somewhere in the wilds — announced to everyone. PvP is legal out there. First come, first served.

🥊

FIGHT DIRTY

Swing an axe, a log, a rock — anything in your hand is a weapon. Source-style movement: bunnyhop, air-strafe, surf the ramps. The city walls are the only law.

HOBOVILLE

A walled safe city with a merchant economy, street lamps that flicker on at dusk, and a live day/night cycle shared by every player on the island.
